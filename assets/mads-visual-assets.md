# MADS Visual Asset Library

This repository contains current MADS / SNTL 84 visual creatives supplied for brand presentation, client communication, digital publishing and campaign planning.

## Recommended asset groups

### Brand & identity
- SNTL 84 branded portraits / phone creatives
- MADS logo and identity graphics

### MADS ONLINE
- Social media / influencer creative
- Digital marketing and performance-oriented visual assets
- Online + offline combined positioning creatives

### MADS DIRECT
- Mobile billboard tricycle creatives
- Surat route / coverage creatives
- Offline advertising solutions creatives

## Brand handling
Use supplied MADS and SNTL 84 marks as the source artwork. Preserve the existing logo proportions, key typography, colours and recognisable identity when adapting the assets for new placements.
