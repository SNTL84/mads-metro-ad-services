# MADS Commercial Model

## MADS has two operating arms

### MADS ONLINE — Digital Arm
Digital marketing and performance services designed to turn attention into measurable digital demand.

**Core services**
- Digital Marketing
- Social Media
- SEO & Indexing / Ranking
- Paid Ads
- Content
- Performance Marketing
- Analytics
- Ecommerce Activation
- Event / Product Campaigns
- Influencer & Niche Business Ads
- Custom Campaign Strategy

### MADS DIRECT — Offline Arm
On-ground advertising designed for visibility across local movement and high-footfall environments.

**Core services**
- Mobile Billboard Tricycle Ads
- Cycle Advertising
- Rickshaw Advertising
- Bus & Transit Ads
- 2-Wheeler / 4-Wheeler Ads
- Newspaper Advertising
- Radio Advertising
- Hoardings & Outdoor Ads
- Leafletting / Card Distribution
- Exhibitions & Events
- Local Services Advertising
