## 3. BUSINESS MODEL SUMMARY (CORRECTED — v2)

EXECUTION SCOPE: 1 business banner ad cycle. Single-unit pilot — prove the
model on one asset before any fleet talk.

### Sequence of operations (in order):
1. COSTING & ASSET PREP
   - Estimate full build cost: cycle purchase/fabrication, cargo frame,
     ad panel mounting hardware, paint/finish, safety fittings.
   - This is a real, itemized cost sheet — every line VERIFIED against
     an actual vendor quote before the pilot launches, not estimated.
2. OPERATOR HIRING
   - Assign the right person on quality, not just availability — define
     a short screening criteria (reliability, route familiarity, basic
     cash handling trust, physical fitness for a 6h pedal shift).
   - Operator comp: ₹14,000/month salary + ₹100/day food allowance
     (paid on top, not folded into the salary figure).
3. CLIENT SIGN-UP
   - Client is signed and creative approved BEFORE the cycle goes live
     on-route — asset prep and hiring happen in parallel with sales,
     but the route doesn't start until a client is contracted.
4. BOOKING SLOT STRUCTURE (replaces the old flat 40-day cycle)
   - 7-day slot
   - 15-day slot
   - 30-day slot
   Each slot is a distinct priced product — shorter slots test client
   appetite and cash flow faster; 30-day slots anchor recurring revenue.
   Pricing per slot length: ASSUMPTION until a rate card is set — flag
   and confirm before quoting a client.

### Updated ownership of costs:
- One-time: cycle build/asset prep (itemized, vendor-quoted).
- Recurring monthly: operator salary ₹14,000 + food ₹100/day (~₹3,000/mo
  at 30 days) = ~₹17,000/month fixed operator cost, independent of how
  many slots are sold that month.
- Variable: print cost per creative change (~₹300–500/change, still
  ASSUMPTION pending vendor confirmation), per earlier printing section.
