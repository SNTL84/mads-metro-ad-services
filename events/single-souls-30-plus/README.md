The campaign is designed around five minimum channels:

1. **Facebook** — organic event awareness and community sharing
2. **Instagram** — reels, posts, stories and profile traffic
3. **Meta Ads** — paid reach, lead/registration conversion and retargeting
4. **Google Form sign-up** — primary registration capture
5. **Event-date promotion** — repeated 11 September / 6 PM / Surat visibility across campaign creatives
