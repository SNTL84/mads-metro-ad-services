## 🎯 Campaign Case Study

**Single Souls 30+** is a community-led event campaign created to move 30+ singles from passive social-media scrolling to real-world conversations, new friendships and meaningful connections.

The campaign is intentionally **not positioned as a dating-app experience or nightclub promotion**. The central proposition is simple:

> **Come single. Leave with a bigger circle. 💫**

### Campaign objectives

- Reach qualified **30+ singles in Surat**.
- Build awareness across **Facebook + Instagram**.
- Use **Meta Ads** as the paid acquisition layer.
- Keep the **Google Form** as the single registration/conversion path.
- Repeat the **11 September · 6 PM · Surat · ₹699** event information consistently.
- Turn campaign attention into an offline community experience.

## 🧩 Event Creative Direction

The supplied **Single Souls 30+** campaign creatives establish a consistent visual system:

- Warm **black / gold / cream** premium palette.
- Circular **Single Souls 30+** identity as the primary recognition mark.
- Human, social and conversational imagery rather than aggressive dating-app imagery.
- Repeated language around **Meet • Connect • Go Beyond**.
- Clear calls to action for **real conversations, real connections and new experiences**.

The event creative set should be treated as one campaign family rather than unrelated posters.
