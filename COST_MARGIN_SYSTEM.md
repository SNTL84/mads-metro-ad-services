## Project Timeline Pricing

Short timeline = higher vendor rates + more of your time → charge higher Extra Markup.

| Timeline | Suggested Extra Markup % | Why | Example on ₹50,000 Cost | Risk |
|----------|--------------------------|-----|-------------------------|------|
| Urgent (1–3 days) | 25–40% | Vendors rush fees + your overtime | Extra ₹12,500–20,000 | High |
| Short (4–7 days) | 18–25% | Limited vendors, faster coordination | Extra ₹9,000–12,500 | Med-High |
| Normal (8–15 days) | 12–18% | Comfortable sourcing + normal overhead | Extra ₹6,000–9,000 | Normal |
| Comfortable (16–30 days) | 10–15% | Best vendor rates, time to negotiate | Extra ₹5,000–7,500 | Low |
| Long (30+ days) | 8–12% | Max negotiation power | Extra ₹4,000–6,000 | Very Low |

### How to Apply
1. Client selects services → get real vendor quotes → enter Vendor Cost.
2. Estimate your Overhead (time + tools + travel + buffer).
3. Keep Min Margin % at 30–40% (do not reduce).
4. Pick Extra Markup % from the Timeline table above.
5. Final price (with GST) calculates automatically.
6. If client negotiates → reduce Extra Markup only. **Never touch Min Margin.**

## How to Use & Rules

### Your Model
- You (SNTL 84) mostly alone + collabs
- Client picks from MADS menu (Cycle, Bus, Google, Robot, Leafleting, etc.)
- You buy assets/services from market vendors
- Pay vendors + add your overhead + Min Margin + Extra Markup (by timeline) + GST → Client Price
- This sheet protects your margin and makes pricing fast & consistent

### Every Project — 7 Steps
1. Open Single Project Costing. Fill Client Name, Dates, Timeline type.
2. Get real vendor quotes → enter in Vendor Cost column.
3. Estimate your Overhead (time, travel, tools, buffer, collab share).
4. Keep Min Margin % at 30–40% (survival margin — do not cut).
5. From Project Timeline Pricing pick Extra Markup %.
6. GST defaults to 18%. Change only if different rate applies.
7. Generate Client Quotation → send via WhatsApp.

### Recommended Min Margin %
| Category | Min Margin % | Note |
|----------|--------------|------|
| Cycle / Rickshaw / 2W / 4W / Leafleting | 35% | High volume possible |
| Bus / Mobile Billboard / Newspaper / Radio | 30–32% | Rate-card / fixed market |
| Exhibition / On-Ground | 35% | Project risk high |
| Social / SEO / Content | 40% | Mostly your time |
| Google / YouTube (mgmt portion) | 15–20% | Ad spend is pass-through |
| Influencer | 30% | Negotiation dependent |
| AI & Robotics | 35% | Logistics + tech risk |

### Final Rule
**Never quote until you have at least one real vendor quote + realistic overhead.**  
Your profit = Client Price (ex-GST) – Vendor Cost – Your Overhead.  
Protect Min Margin. Use Extra Markup as negotiation buffer.
